Strategic Typing Game NyctoType Releases Public Demo on Steam

BogosorGames released a public demo of its strategic typing game NyctoType, currently in development, on Steam on September 30, 2026.

ABOUT NYCTOTYPE

NyctoType is a strategic typing game in which players complete typing challenges to move pieces on a board and create new ones. Victory depends not only on typing speed, but also on deciding what to create or move based on each piece's different creation and movement costs, and when to use its abilities. The project was selected for the first cohort of TGCA (Top Game Creators Academy) grant projects and is being developed with support from the program.

Select one of your pieces and a direction, then enter the displayed text to move it one step. New pieces are created by selecting them in the console and completing a typing challenge. Sending a piece off the opponent's edge of the board deals damage. The first player to reduce the opponent's health to zero wins.

BOARD TACTICS BUILT AROUND PIECE-SPECIFIC ABILITIES

Pieces differ in movement cost, creation cost, movement directions, and abilities. Players choose their actions based on typing speed, the state of the board, and effects such as destroying or pushing opposing pieces and increasing damage.

A FIRST-PERSON VIEW OF THE ENTIRE BOARD

Players face their opponent across a table and battle from a board-game-like perspective. They survey the entire board to decide where to attack and where to defend.

HELPING TYPING GAMES GROW AS AN ESPORT

Through NyctoType, BogosorGames aims to help typing games grow into an esport that more people play, watch, and take part in.
