## About

The only controls are press to jump and hold to long jump.
The traces of those two actions are converted into text as Morse code.

At first, you can move with some freedom.
As time passes, survival depends on controlling the stage so that you keep saying "I AM SO HAPPY."

A one-button runner like modern society, where even if you are not happy, you have to say you are happy to survive.
